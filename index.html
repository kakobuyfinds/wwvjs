From product link to warehouse QC

  1. Verify the listing. Open the CAFANS product page, follow its original seller link and check that OrientDig accepts the URL. Record the exact size, color and quantity before requesting a quote.
  2. Use comparable measurements. For shoes, confirm whether the chart measures foot or insole length. For tops, compare the flat chest width and shoulder width against a garment you own.
  3. Review the received item. If warehouse QC photos are available for your order, match them against your selected variant. Ask for a ruler photo if an important measurement is missing and check the return window before approving dispatch.
  4. Compare shipping on the same basis. Use your destination, packed weight and parcel dimensions for each quote. Check the billable weight and whether the quote includes insurance, taxes or handling.

OrientDig spreadsheet FAQ

What is an OrientDig spreadsheet?

It is a product shortlist used to compare seller listings before sending a purchase request through OrientDig. This directory sources its picks and reference prices from CAFANS.

Where do I find the original seller URL?

Open a product’s CAFANS listing and look for the original marketplace link. Copy that URL and confirm that OrientDig recognizes it and shows the correct item.

How do I export my shortlist?

Choose Save on the product cards, then select Download shortlist CSV. The file includes the product name, category, USD estimate and CAFANS listing URL.

What should I confirm before paying?

Confirm the current price and availability for your chosen variant, the agent’s purchase and return terms, and which charges are included in the shipping quote.
